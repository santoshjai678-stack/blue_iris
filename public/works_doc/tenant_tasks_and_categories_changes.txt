1. SUMMARY:
-----------
Implemented the complete Task & Checklist Engine in the Tenant Administration portal:
- Task Categories CRUD: Custom task groupings (Opening Checklist, Cash Count, Sanitization, Expiry, Security) with display ordering, status toggle, and question counts.
- Daily Survey Tasks & Questions CRUD: Full question builder with:
  * Category assignment
  * Target designation / role filtering (e.g. Store Manager, Stylist)
  * Target Store assignment (Universal / All stores or specific store branch)
  * Survey Frequency (Daily, Weekly, Monthly)
  * Task Tagging (General, Cash, Attrition, Expiry)
  * Response Control Types: Radio (Yes/No or Single Choice), Checkbox (Multi-select), and Text/Numeric Input
  * Dynamic Answer Options Builder
  * Daily Shift Slot Timings (Morning Opening, Afternoon Shift, Evening Closing)
  * Mandatory & "Allow Other" toggles.
